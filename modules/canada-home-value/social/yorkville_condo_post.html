YORKVILLE · TORONTO

Problem:

A $1,880,000 Yorkville condo is bought with 25% down ($470,000) and a $1,410,000 interest-only mortgage. Suppose its market value follows geometric Brownian motion
$$dP_t=\mu P_t\,dt+\sigma P_t\,dW_t.$$
Let \(\tau=\inf\{t\ge0:P_t\le 1{,}410{,}000\}\) be the first time the condo is in negative equity. Find \(P(\tau\le5)\).

Solution:

\(X_t=\ln\!\big(\tfrac{P_t}{P_0}\big)=mt+\sigma W_t,\quad m=\mu-\tfrac12\sigma^2,\qquad b=\ln\!\big(\tfrac{1.41\text{M}}{1.88\text{M}}\big)=\ln(0.75)\)
First passage of Brownian motion with drift:
$$P(\tau\le T)=\Phi\!\Big(\tfrac{b-mT}{\sigma\sqrt T}\Big)+e^{2mb/\sigma^2}\,\Phi\!\Big(\tfrac{b+mT}{\sigma\sqrt T}\Big)$$
With \(\mu=3\%,\ \sigma=12\%,\ T=5\): \(m=0.0228,\ b=-0.2877,\ \sigma\sqrt T=0.2683\)
\(\tfrac{b-mT}{\sigma\sqrt T}=-1.4970\)\(\tfrac{b+mT}{\sigma\sqrt T}=-0.6473\)
\(P(\tau\le5)=\Phi(-1.4970)+e^{-0.9110}\,\Phi(-0.6473)\approx0.0672+0.1040\approx0.171\)
P(Yorkville condo underwater within 5 years) ≈ 17.1%
