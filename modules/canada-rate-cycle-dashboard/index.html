Canada's Rate Cycle, 2015 to 2026

How fast did borrowing costs follow the Bank of Canada, and did higher rates actually cool inflation? Built by John Khan with Bank of Canada data.

Pick a window and every chart and summary card updates.

1. Policy rate vs inflation

The Bank started raising in March 2022, a year after inflation broke above its 3% ceiling. Inflation peaked at 8.1% in June 2022 and was back under 3% by early 2024.

2. Policy rate vs 5-year conventional mortgage

The posted 5-year rate moved far less than the policy rate. The policy rate rose 4.75 points from 2022 to 2023, the mortgage rate about 2 points, and it has stayed near 6% even after cuts.

3. Real policy rate (policy rate minus inflation)

Policy was deeply negative in real terms through 2021 and 2022. It only turned positive in 2023, which lines up with when inflation began to come down for good.

4. US dollar in Canadian dollars

The loonie weakened as the Bank cut ahead of the US Federal Reserve in 2024, with USD/CAD moving from about 1.35 to above 1.43 in early 2025.
