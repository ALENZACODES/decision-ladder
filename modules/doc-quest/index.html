          Where this list comes from

          The lender list on the first screen is a combined checklist I put together from the documents Canadian lenders commonly ask for. It isn't copied from any one lender. These public pages back it up:

          • Getting preapproved for a mortgage, Financial Consumer Agency of Canada: ID, proof of employment, down payment, debts, and two years of notices of assessment if self-employed.
          • What to bring to your lending appointment, BMO: two IDs for a purchase, a T1 General with its notice of assessment for self-employed or variable income, the signed offer and the listing.
          • Mortgage pre-approval checklist, Access Credit Union: letter of employment with a pay stub, and a 90-day history of the down payment funds.
          • The Home Buyers' Plan, Canada Revenue Agency: the $60,000 withdrawal limit.

          Lenders differ, and yours may ask for something extra or skip something here. This list covers the usual asks from Canadian lenders for a home purchase. It isn't advice.
