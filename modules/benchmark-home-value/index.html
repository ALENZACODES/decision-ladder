How close would this page have been?

Every assessed home with a history in Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver and the rest of Alberta and British Columbia was priced from each earlier assessment it had, using only what was known at that time and this page's own price index, then checked against what the city assessed later. Every street in Canada was also typed back into the address box to see which home the page lands on.
